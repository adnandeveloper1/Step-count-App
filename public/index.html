1. Introduction

The Build Up mobile application is developed and operated by Nex Appra (Developer: Moiz Ur Rehman). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service, and the choices you have with respect to that data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect your email address for account authentication. We also collect health and biometric metrics necessary to provide core fitness features, including:

  • Step count and activity data (via device sensors and Health Connect)
  • Height, weight, and body fat percentage
  • Precise location data, collected while you record an outdoor activity, in order to map your route, calculate distance, and display your activity history

We also automatically collect limited technical and usage data through analytics tools, described in Section 9.

Location data: Precise location is only collected during an active, user-initiated outdoor tracking session. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; doing so will disable route-mapping features but will not affect other parts of the app.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the UK, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Consent: Height, weight, body fat percentage, and other biometric/health data are "special category data" under Article 9 GDPR. We only collect and process this data with your explicit, freely given consent, obtained when you first enter this information in the app. You may withdraw this consent at any time in Settings, which will stop further collection and let you request deletion of previously collected data.
  • Consent: Precise location data is collected only with your explicit permission grant at the device level, and only while you actively record an outdoor session. You may revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Contract: We process your email address and account information because it's necessary to provide the app's core service to you.
  • Legitimate interest: We use limited technical/usage data (Section 9) to keep the app secure, functioning, and to understand aggregate usage patterns, in each case balanced against your privacy interests.

4. How Data Is Used

Your biometric metrics are used strictly to calculate stride length, distance covered, and active caloric burn. We sync this data securely using Firebase to maintain your activity history across sessions. We do not use your health or biometric data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the app's fitness features, and we do not combine it with advertising identifiers.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal and biometric data for as long as your account remains active, so the app can function correctly across sessions.

Data type Retention period
Account & email Until account deletion
Biometric/health metrics Until account deletion or until you remove specific entries
Precise location data Until account deletion, or until you delete individual recorded activities
Analytics/usage data Up to 14 months, per Google Analytics for Firebase defaults

If you delete your account, we permanently delete your personal and biometric data from our active systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party providers to operate and maintain the application:

  • Google Play Services — core platform services required to run the app on Android.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — collects device information, app usage events, and approximate location (derived from IP) to help us understand how the app is used. This does not include your biometric data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore — used to authenticate your account and store your app data securely.
  • Health Connect by Google — used, with your permission, to read and write activity data between Build Up and other health apps on your device.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy and applicable data processing terms; we do not permit these providers to use your data for their own independent purposes.

7. International Data Transfers

Our service providers, including Firebase and Google Analytics, may process and store data on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Where we transfer personal data from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to a country not deemed to provide an adequate level of protection, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, as implemented by our providers (e.g., Google's SCC framework for Firebase and Google Analytics).

8. Security Measures

We apply industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls limiting who can view stored data, and reliance on Firebase's built-in security infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The app does not use browser cookies, but Google Analytics for Firebase uses device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID, where available) to distinguish users and measure app usage. You can limit this by:

  • Resetting or opting out of your device's advertising ID in your device settings
  • Disabling Analytics data collection for the app in Settings within Build Up

10. Your Rights Under GDPR (EEA/UK Users)

You retain full rights to inspect, rectify, or request permanent deletion of your stored personal information. Specifically, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal
  • Request restriction of, or object to, certain processing
  • Request a portable copy of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

You can initiate data deletion directly within the application settings or by contacting us. We do not sell or rent personal information to any third parties.

11. Your Rights Under CCPA/CPRA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information (including sensitive personal information, which covers your biometric and health data) we collect, use, and disclose
  • Delete personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information, including biometric and health metrics, to purposes necessary to provide the app's core features
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights
We do not sell your personal information. We also do not "share" it, as that term is defined under the CPRA, for cross-context behavioral advertising.

You can exercise these rights by contacting us at the email below. We will verify your request using your account email before fulfilling it.

12. Children's Privacy

Build Up is not directed at children under the age of 13, and biometric/health features specifically are intended only for users aged 16 and older, consistent with heightened protections for sensitive health data in several jurisdictions. We do not knowingly collect personal, health, or biometric information from individuals under these age thresholds. If we learn we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the app or by email, and will update the "Effective Date" above.
